Copykeep

A clipboard history for macOS that works like Windows' Win+V. Press ⌥V in any app and a small panel opens at the text cursor with everything you copied recently. Pick one and it's pasted.

Download for Mac
Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · All releases

Install

  1. Unzip and move it to Applications

    Open the downloaded Copykeep.zip and drag Copykeep into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it once past Gatekeeper

    The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time macOS says it can't check it for malware. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Copykeep. You only do this once.

  3. Allow Accessibility

    The app asks for Accessibility on its first launch. It needs it to press ⌘V for you and to find the text cursor. Without it, choosing a clip only copies it and the panel opens at the mouse.

  4. Press ⌥V

    Copykeep lives in the menu bar. Turn on Open at login in its Settings (⌘, in the panel).

Or install from Terminal, which skips step 2:

curl -sL https://github.com/stanimir93/copykeep/releases/latest/download/Copykeep.zip -o /tmp/cs.zip && ditto -xk /tmp/cs.zip /Applications && open /Applications/Copykeep.app

What it does

Opens at the text cursor

Below the caret, or above it when there's no room. At the mouse when an app doesn't report its caret.

Search as you type

Every word must appear in the clip or the name of the app it came from. Newest first.

Pin what you reuse

Pinned clips stay on top and are never removed, by the history limit or by Clear.

Keeps the formatting

Text, RTF, HTML, links, images and files. ⇧↩ pastes plain text instead.

Passwords stay out

Nothing copied in 1Password, Bitwarden, Passwords, Keychain Access and other password managers is kept.

Secrets are hidden

API keys, tokens and password-like strings show as •••••••• in the list and still paste in full.

Tabs

All, Pinned, Text, Images, Links and Shortcuts. Tab and ⇧Tab switch between them.

Text shortcuts

Type -email and a space in any app and it becomes your email address. Make them in the Shortcuts tab, or in a JSON file a script or an agent can write.

Stays on your Mac

The history is kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Copykeep. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Keys

⌥VOpen or close the panel (change it in Settings)
↑ ↓Move the selection
↩ or a clickPaste
⇧↩Paste as plain text
⌘1 – ⌘9Paste the first nine
⌘PPin or unpin
⌘⌫Delete the clip or shortcut
⌘N, ⌘ENew shortcut, edit the selected one
EscClear the search, or close
